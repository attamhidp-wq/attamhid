Diploma in Arabic for Non-Native Speakers

A two-year foundation programme (four semesters, 84 credit hours) that builds reading, writing, listening, speaking, grammar, morphology, lexicography, rhetoric and translation in Arabic, alongside courses in the Qur’an, Hadith, Fiqh, Creed and Seerah, ending with a graduation project and field training.

Level 1

Reading Skills 1, Writing Skills 1, Listening and Speaking 1, Vocabulary and Expressions, Arabic Syntax 1, Arabic Phonetics and Pronunciation, Linguistic Structures, The Holy Quran 1 (Recitation and Tajweed)

Level 2

Reading Skills 2, Writing Skills 2, Listening and Speaking 2, Arabic Syntax 2, Arabic Morphology 1, Arabic Lexicography and Dictionary Skills, The Holy Quran 2 (Recitation and Exegesis of Juz' Amma), Simplified Jurisprudence of Worship

Level 3

Advanced Reading, Written Expression, Linguistic Communication, Extensive Reading, Arabic Syntax 3, Arabic Morphology 2 (Derivation and Patterns), Arabic Rhetoric 1, Prophetic Hadith (An-Nawawi's Forty), The Prophetic Biography

Level 4

Literary Texts, Linguistic Editing, Public Speaking and Media in Arabic, Translation (Arabic – English), Arabic Rhetoric 2 (Quranic Rhetoric), Linguistic Analysis of Texts, Islamic Creed (Al-Aqidah Al-Tahawiyyah), Graduation Project and Field Training
