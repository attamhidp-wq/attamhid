General Diploma in Arabic for Non-Native Speakers (Full-time)

A two-year, full-time foundation programme (four semesters, 80 credit hours) that builds reading, writing, listening, speaking, grammar and morphology in Arabic, alongside courses in the Qur’an, Hadith, Fiqh, Seerah and Islamic history, ending with a graduation project.

Level 1

Reading Skills 1, Writing Skills 1, Listening and Speaking 1, Arabic Syntax 1, Linguistic Structures, The Holy Quran 1 (Recitation and Tajweed), Jurisprudence of Worship

Level 2

Reading Skills 2, Writing Skills 2, Listening and Speaking 2, Arabic Syntax 2, Arabic Morphology, The Holy Quran 2, Prophetic Hadith (An-Nawawi's Forty), The Prophetic Biography

Level 3

Advanced Reading, Written Expression, Linguistic Communication, Arabic Syntax 3, Quranic Rhetoric, Quranic Exegesis (Juz' Amma), Islamic Creed (Al-Aqidah Al-Tahawiyyah), Jurisprudence of Transactions

Level 4

Literary Texts, Linguistic Editing, Public Speaking and Oratory, Translation (Arabic – English), Prophetic Hadith (Riyad As-Salihin), Islamic History (The Rightly Guided Caliphs), Digital Communication and Media in Arabic, Graduation Project
